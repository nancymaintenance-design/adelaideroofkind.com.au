Before you contact us

Details that help us respond.

  1. Tell us the suburb, road or closest cross street.
  2. Describe what you can see: a leak mark, displaced tile, debris, overflow or another roof concern.
  3. Let us know whether the issue is active or changed after recent weather.
  4. Do not climb onto the roof to collect more information.

Prepare your enquiry

Details for an Unley roof enquiry.

Give your Unley address and road or closest cross street, such as Unley Road if it matches your location.

For gutter overflow, describe the section affected and whether water appears at a joint, outlet or downpipe. Mention visible debris separately from a leak or damaged fitting so cleaning and repair questions can be assessed individually.

Describe only what you can see from ground level; do not climb onto the roof. Include the roof material if known and any access constraints, such as restricted entry or shared access.
