Common questions

Goodwood roof repair questions.

What should I do after noticing a roof leak?

Record where water first became visible and move valuables away from the affected area where safe. Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange a roof inspection. Stay on the ground; our team arranges safe access and checks the affected roof area.

Can planned maintenance prevent every leak?

No maintenance plan removes all risk, but condition checks can identify visible issues before they become more disruptive.

Do you work with tiled roofs?

Yes. We replace cracked or slipped tiles and repair related ridge and flashing defects. We check tile profiles and overlaps on site before confirming the repair scope and written quote.
