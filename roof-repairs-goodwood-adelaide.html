Common questions

Goodwood roof repair questions.

What should I do after noticing a roof leak?

Record where water first became visible, move valuables away from the affected area and contact a professional rather than climbing onto the roof.

Can planned maintenance prevent every leak?

No maintenance plan removes all risk, but condition checks can identify visible issues before they become more disruptive.

Do you work with tiled roofs?

Yes. Tile position, cracks, overlaps and surrounding roof details can all be part of an inspection-based assessment.
