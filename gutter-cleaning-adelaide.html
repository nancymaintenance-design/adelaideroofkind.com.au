What our gutter cleaning service includes

We agree the gutter runs, outlets and access arrangements before starting. Our team removes accumulated leaves, sediment and accessible debris, then reviews the drainage path and visible gutter condition. This gives you a clear account of the areas cleaned and any remaining issue needing attention.

  • Debris removal from the agreed gutter runs and accessible outlets
  • Checks of accessible downpipe flow and remaining obstructions
  • Observation of visible joints, supports and outlet alignment
  • Removal of the debris collected during the work
  • Advice on maintenance based on the debris load at your home

How Ellis plans and completes the clean

1. Confirm the property and access

Tell us the suburb, number of storeys, surrounding trees and where overflow occurs. Ellis plans safe access around the building, roof height and agreed work areas. We confirm the scope and quote so you know what is included.

2. Clear the agreed gutter runs and outlets

Our team removes the accumulated debris and gives particular attention to collection points around outlets. We review accessible downpipe flow as part of the agreed work. Concealed drainage routes and additional repairs are identified separately, not silently added to the cleaning bill.

3. Explain the condition and next steps

Cleaning reveals the condition of the gutter beneath the build-up. Ellis explains visible damage, remaining restrictions and maintenance needs. A cracked joint or loose bracket is a repair issue; we provide a separate repair scope when it needs work.

Cleaning or repair: which service do you need?

Choose cleaning when leaves and sediment are restricting the drainage path. Choose an assessment of the gutter or downpipe when water leaks through a joint, a section sags, a connection separates or overflow continues after debris has been removed. Ellis can identify both needs during the visit and itemise the work clearly.

When to arrange gutter maintenance

Look from ground level for leaves near outlets, overflow during rain and staining around eaves. Nearby trees and seasonal leaf fall increase the debris load. After cleaning, we recommend a maintenance interval suited to what we found rather than the same timetable for every property.

Do not climb onto a roof or use an unstable ladder to check a blockage. Ellis plans the access and carries out the agreed maintenance work. For the causes of persistent overflow, read why gutters overflow and what to check. If water is appearing indoors, see our roof leak repair service.

Gutter cleaning FAQs

What does gutter cleaning include?

Ellis removes leaves, sediment and accessible debris from the agreed gutter runs and outlets, reviews accessible downpipe flow, and explains any damage revealed by cleaning.

Will cleaning fix a leaking gutter joint?

Cleaning removes debris. A leaking joint, damaged bracket or misaligned section needs a repair. Ellis identifies the problem and itemises repair work separately.

How often should I book gutter cleaning?

The right interval depends on nearby trees, seasonal leaf fall, roof height and the debris found during the clean. Ellis recommends a schedule based on your property rather than a fixed interval for every home.

Can you clean gutters on a two-storey home?

Ellis plans access around roof height, pitch and the agreed gutter runs. Tell us about the building and access when requesting a quote so we can plan the work.
