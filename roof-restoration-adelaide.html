Roof restoration Adelaide

Roof restoration brings preparation, repairs and protective finishing into one clear scope of work.

Restoration begins with condition

A clear restoration scope considers roof material, visible defects, drainage, structural condition and the work required.

A restoration scope includes

  • Cleaning and preparation
  • Tile, ridge-capping, flashing or sheet repairs
  • Repointing
  • Protective coating
  • Clear scope of work

Compare written scopes, not slogans

When comparing quotes, look for the specific repair steps, materials, preparation, access requirements, exclusions and warranty terms actually offered in writing.
