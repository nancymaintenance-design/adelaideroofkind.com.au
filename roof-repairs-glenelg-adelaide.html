Why is ongoing roof maintenance useful?

Regular condition checks can identify visible deterioration, drainage issues or displaced components before they become more disruptive.

Can you inspect tiles and flashings together?

Yes. The roof covering and its transitions need to be considered together when a leak or maintenance issue is reported.
