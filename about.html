Our story

We want to reset the standard for home repairs.

Established in November 2020, Ellis Services Group began with a straightforward belief: homeowners deserve honest advice, fair pricing and support after the work is finished.

For many people, home repair and renovation can feel uncertain—especially when the problem is urgent or difficult to understand. We aim to make the process clearer: explain the issue in plain language, propose an appropriate scope of work and treat every home with care.

Experience that fits Australian homes

Our team brings practical experience, modern equipment and familiarity with Australian homes and relevant repair standards. We work with homeowners and have built ongoing relationships with property agencies that value clear communication and dependable service.

Planning around the property

What homeowners can expect.

Every roof is different. We check these practical factors on site when defining the repair scope and written quote.

01

Existing roof conditions

Weathering, previous repairs, broken or displaced tiles, ridge condition, flashing and drainage all affect what needs closer inspection. Visible symptoms are useful, but they are not always the whole story.

02

Weather, access and timing

Ellis plans safe access, weather timing, roof pitch and property use before scheduling the repair work. We discuss these details early rather than treating them as an afterthought.

03

Materials and repair compatibility

For older Adelaide roofs, Ellis checks tile profiles, ridge capping, flashings and surrounding materials, then completes a repair plan matched to the roof damage.

Good to know

Company and enquiry questions.

For help at your property, contact us with the suburb and a brief description. Photos are optional and can be sent by email.

What should I send with a new enquiry?

Send the property suburb, your preferred contact details and the roof issue you can see. For a leak, mention where water appears inside and whether it follows rain or wind. A description is enough; photos by email are optional.

Will the quote explain the proposed work?

Yes. Our written quote explains the observed issue, the proposed repair work, materials, access requirements and any exclusions or assumptions. We confirm changes to the agreed scope before additional work proceeds.

When Ellis Services Group arranges your roof repair plan

Some property work requires regulated trades or specialist services. Ellis identifies those requirements and confirms the responsible qualified contracting arrangements with you before booking the work.
