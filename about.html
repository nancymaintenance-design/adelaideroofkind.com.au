How an enquiry moves forward

Clear details before work begins.

A good repair conversation starts with the property, the visible issue and the practical details that affect access, timing and scope.

01

Start with the issue

Tell us your Adelaide suburb, what you have noticed and when it first appeared. Clear photos of the roof area, ceiling marks, gutters or damaged tiles can help us understand the enquiry before a visit is arranged.

02

Planning around the property

What homeowners can expect.

Every roof is different. These are the practical factors that help shape a sensible repair discussion.

01

Existing roof conditions

Weathering, previous repairs, broken or displaced tiles, ridge condition, flashing and drainage all affect what needs closer inspection. Visible symptoms are useful, but they are not always the whole story.

02

Weather, access and timing

Ellis plans safe access, weather timing, roof pitch and property use before scheduling the repair work. We discuss these details early rather than treating them as an afterthought.

03

Materials and repair compatibility

For older Adelaide roofs, Ellis checks tile profiles, ridge capping, flashings and surrounding materials, then completes a repair plan matched to the roof damage.
