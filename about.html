Our story

We want to reset the standard for home repairs.

Established in November 2020, Ellis Services Group began with a straightforward belief: homeowners deserve honest advice, fair pricing and support after the work is finished.

For many people, home repair and renovation can feel uncertain—especially when the problem is urgent or difficult to understand. We aim to make the process clearer: explain the issue in plain language, propose an appropriate scope of work and treat every home with care.

Experience that fits Australian homes

Our team brings practical experience, modern equipment and familiarity with Australian homes and relevant repair standards. We work with homeowners and have built ongoing relationships with property agencies that value clear communication and dependable service.

What guides our work

  • Integrity first: give customers straightforward information before work begins.
  • Fair value: define the proposed work clearly so homeowners can make an informed decision.
  • Service that lasts: earn trust through quality work, communication and responsible follow-up.
