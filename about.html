How an enquiry moves forward

Roof Inspections and Written Quotes

Contact us with the property location and visible issue. We confirm an on-site appointment and plan safe access before inspecting the roof.

01

Describe the Roof Leak or Visible Damage

02

Planning around the property

Roof Condition, Access and Repair Materials

Every roof is different. We check these practical factors on site when defining the repair scope and written quote.

01

Existing Tiles, Ridges, Flashing and Drainage

Weathering, previous repairs, broken or displaced tiles, ridge condition, flashing and drainage all affect what needs closer inspection. Visible symptoms are useful, but they are not always the whole story.

02

Roof Pitch, Weather and Property Access

We consider safe access, suitable weather, roof pitch and how the property is used when scheduling repairs. We discuss these arrangements with you before work is booked.

03

Tile Profiles, Ridge Caps and Repair Compatibility

For older roofs, we check tile profiles, ridge capping, flashings and adjoining materials so the repair plan suits the existing roof.

Company and enquiry questions.

For help at your property, contact us with the suburb and a brief description. Photos are optional and can be sent by email.

What should I send with a new enquiry?

Send the property suburb, your preferred contact details and the roof issue you can see. For a leak, mention where water appears inside and whether it follows rain or wind. A description is enough; photos by email are optional.

Will the quote explain the proposed work?

Yes. Our written quote explains the observed issue, the proposed repair work, materials, access requirements and any exclusions or assumptions. We confirm changes to the agreed scope before additional work proceeds.

When Ellis Services Group arranges your roof repair plan

If the proposed work needs a regulated trade or specialist, we explain the requirements and confirm the responsible contracting arrangements with you before booking the work.
