Roof cleaning is not gutter cleaning or roof repair

Roof surface cleaning addresses the agreed surface areas and deposits. Gutter cleaning concerns debris in gutters and accessible outlets. A leak, broken tile or failed flashing requires its own assessment rather than an assumption that washing will fix it.

If overflowing gutters are your main concern, see our gutter cleaning and accessible outlet clearing service. If water is entering the house, use roof leak investigation and repairs. You can book a roof condition inspection to clarify the next step before choosing a larger scope.

Tile roof cleaning: confirm the material and finish

Dirty tiled roofs can have leaf debris, surface marking and visible growth in different areas. Those observations help plan an assessment; they do not identify the tile manufacturer, establish the cause of a leak or prove the tiles need repainting. Tell us the tile type if you know it, along with any previous coating work.

Concrete and terracotta are not the same cleaning job

The cleaning proposal should account for the actual tile and its condition. Terracotta roof cleaning should not automatically be bundled with painting. Monier's roof cleaning and maintenance advice cautions against pressure washers for its tiles and explains that terracotta colour is fired into the material. Its guidance is not a universal specification for every roof brand.

Metal and Colorbond roof surfaces

For metal roofs, distinguish deposits from coating damage or corrosion before agreeing to surface work. The existing finish and relevant manufacturer's care instructions matter. See Colorbond steel maintenance guidance for manufacturer information. Suspected defects can be assessed through our metal and Colorbond roof repairs service.

Roof Moss and Lichen Removal: Material and Method Checks

A shaded roof with green growth, a roof with patchy pale marks and a roof covered in leaves may need different attention. Describe what you see from a safe location. The condition and material determine the appropriate proposal; a photograph does not establish that a particular chemical or washing pressure is suitable.

If you are comparing soft washing and pressure cleaning, ask what method is proposed for your material, why it is suitable and how nearby areas will be protected. No single method is promised for all roofs. Unknown or suspect material must be identified before work proceeds. Follow Asbestos SA's safety guidance; suspected asbestos is not a routine roof-cleaning job.

Plan access, roof equipment and runoff

Tell us about storeys, steep or restricted roof areas, solar panels, roof vents, skylights, rainwater tanks and nearby gardens. These features affect access and protection planning. Roof cleaning does not include solar electrical work or equipment removal unless separately arranged with the appropriate people.

For a roof connected to a rainwater tank, agree the catchment protection, runoff handling and reinstatement arrangements before cleaning. A clean-looking roof is not a water-quality test. Your quote should also distinguish roof-surface work, valley debris removal, gutters, outlets and disposal rather than assume one price includes everything.

Roof cleaning FAQs

Can I book roof cleaning without painting or restoration?

Yes. Roof cleaning can be separately scoped after assessment of the material, condition and access. Painting, repairs and gutter work are included only where agreed in the written quote.

Is roof cleaning the same as gutter cleaning?

No. Cleaning roof surfaces and clearing gutters or outlets are different tasks. Check the written scope to see which areas, waste removal and related work are included.

Is soft washing or pressure cleaning suitable for every roof?

No. The material, existing finish, condition and relevant manufacturer guidance determine suitability. Ask for the proposed method and protection arrangements before agreeing to work.

Does moss or a dark roof mark prove that the roof is leaking?

No. Surface appearance alone does not establish a leak or its source. If water is entering the property, arrange a separate assessment of the water path and roof condition.

Can a roof connected to a rainwater tank be assessed for cleaning?

Yes. Tell us about the connection when booking so the scope can address catchment protection, runoff handling and reinstatement arrangements. Cleaning is not a water-quality certification.
