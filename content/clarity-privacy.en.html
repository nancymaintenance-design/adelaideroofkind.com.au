Privacy and Clarity preferences

This page explains optional Microsoft Clarity analytics on this website.

Microsoft Clarity

Clarity stays off until you allow it. With permission, it records masked reading, scrolling and clicks for heatmaps and session recordings, and may process device, connection and first-party analytics identifiers.

Page text is masked. Contact, confirmation and staff pages, and visits containing query strings or fragments, are excluded. Recording stops when you begin using a form or editable field. Form content and unsent drafts are excluded.

Use Clarity preferences at the foot of the page to change your choice. Refusing does not affect browsing or using the website. Browser no-tracking signals keep Clarity off. This preference controls Clarity only.

Microsoft provides Clarity and processes the analytics data when you allow it. For questions about analytics on this website, contact Ellis Services Group.
