Assess the defect before choosing the repair

A leaking metal roof does not automatically require a full replacement or a repaint. An on-site assessment considers the sheet profile and condition, accessible fixings, laps, roof-to-wall junctions, penetrations and drainage. A ceiling stain alone does not establish which component has failed. Describe when the leak occurs so the inspection can consider the water path rather than only the mark inside.

Roof screws, washers and sheet laps

Leaks around roof fixings, perished roof screw washers and a metal roof lap leak are different concerns. The assessment checks what can be observed and whether compatible replacement parts or other repairs are appropriate. Tightening every fixing or adding sealant without identifying the defect is not a substitute for a repair plan. Do not climb onto the roof to test screws yourself.

Surface rust, corrosion and local sheet damage

Surface rust and corrosion through a roof sheet need different decisions. The written scope should identify the affected area, whether repair or local sheet replacement is proposed, and the limits of matching an existing profile or finish. Painting over unsound metal is not the same as restoring its condition. Replacement feasibility depends on the actual roof, not just the visible size of a stain.

Colorbond roofing and the existing finish

For Colorbond roof repairs, tell us the material and colour if known, along with previous work. A coating mark may require a different response from a damaged sheet, fixing or flashing. Material identification and compatibility are part of deciding the work. The material name does not imply that Ellis is a manufacturer-authorised contractor.

Compare repair and appearance work separately: our roof painting and maintenance service explains preparation and coating scope, while roof flashing repair considerations explain junction defects.

Common situations to describe when booking

  • A corrugated roof leak that appears during heavy rain or wind-driven rain.
  • Water entering where an extension or patio roof joins the house.
  • A previous inspection mentioning worn washers, corrosion or sheet damage.
  • A leak appearing near solar mounts after equipment work, without assuming the equipment caused it.

Tell us about solar panels and other roof equipment before booking. Roof work and electrical or equipment work are separate responsibilities. Any necessary equipment access or specialist involvement must be agreed, not assumed to be included.

What a metal roof repair quote should explain

The assessment leads to a written quote identifying the proposed repair area, materials, access arrangements and exclusions. Whole-roof replacement is not the default alternative to a small repair. If conditions prevent a suitable local repair, the reasons and next steps should be explained before work proceeds.

  • Inspection access and any areas that could not be assessed.
  • The agreed repair or maintenance tasks and compatible components.
  • Whether preparation, waste removal or specialist access is included.
  • The written workmanship terms applicable to that particular work.

Metal roof repairs FAQs

Can one damaged metal roof sheet be replaced instead of the whole roof?

Sometimes. The sheet condition, profile, adjoining details and access need assessment before local replacement can be confirmed. The quote should explain the proposed area and any matching limitations.

Do leaks around roof screws always mean the washers need replacing?

No. Fixings and washers are among the components to check, but laps, flashing and other defects can also allow water entry. The repair scope should follow the findings rather than assume one cause.

Can repainting fix a leaking Colorbond roof?

A new finish does not replace defective sheets, failed fixings or flashing repairs. Assess the defect first and compare repair work separately from painting.

Can you assess a leak near solar panels?

Describe the location and timing when booking. Roof access and any necessary equipment or electrical work must be coordinated separately; the position of a leak does not prove its cause.
