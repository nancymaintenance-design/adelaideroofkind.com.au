Start with the question you want the inspection to answer

You may be investigating a new ceiling stain, checking a weathered roof before maintenance, or comparing a large repair proposal with a more targeted option. Tell us the purpose when booking. A roof condition inspection and a leak investigation can overlap, but they are not automatically the same task.

For active water entry, our roof leak assessment and repairs service focuses on the symptoms and potential water path. For general condition, the inspection scope identifies which roof areas can be checked and what information is needed for the next decision.

What the roof condition assessment considers

Roof covering, ridges and junctions

Accessible observations can include roof tiles or metal sheets, ridge lines, visible flashing junctions and penetrations. The inspector considers the actual material and condition, rather than treating age, faded colour or surface growth as proof that the whole roof needs replacement.

Drainage and equipment surroundings

Valleys, gutters and outlets can be relevant to the concern, along with roof areas beside chimneys, skylights, vents and solar mounts. Their inclusion and access must be agreed. An observation near equipment does not prove the equipment caused a leak or mean electrical or product work is included.

The photographs above show roof condition views across these kinds of areas. They help explain what can be documented, but do not establish hidden condition, tile composition or the cause of a particular problem.

Photos, written scope and inspection limitations

If you need roof condition photos or a written report, say so before booking. Confirm the required format, level of detail, accessible areas and any limits in the quote. Do not assume that a repair quotation, a photo record and a specialist report are interchangeable.

  • The purpose of the visit and the roof areas included.
  • Access arrangements and how inaccessible areas will be recorded.
  • Whether findings are explained on site, supplied as photographs or provided in writing.
  • Whether a repair quote is included or prepared separately.
  • The fee and any further visits or specialist work that are outside the scope.

A roof inspection is not automatically a structural certification, a whole-building pre-purchase inspection or an insurer-approved report. If another party needs specific evidence, provide its requirements before booking so suitability can be confirmed. No sample report or insurance approval is implied by this photo gallery.

Leaks after rain, damp ceilings and condensation

Note when moisture appears: during heavy rain, wind-driven rain, after equipment work or on cold mornings without recent rain. These patterns are useful background, not a remote diagnosis. A ceiling water stain can have more than one possible source and may not sit directly below the entry point.

Read our condensation or rainwater entry explanation for safe observations to share. Do not enter an unsafe roof space, climb onto the roof or disturb materials to investigate.

Use the findings to choose the next step

A defined inspection helps separate cleaning, localised repairs and broader finishing work. Ask what observation supports each proposed task and which areas remain uncertain. Repair work should have its own agreed scope; booking an inspection does not commit you to a full restoration.

For visible surface growth, compare our roof cleaning assessment and scope. For damaged components, see roof repairs and maintenance. If appearance work is being considered, our roof painting service explains material suitability and repair-before-finish questions.

Roof inspection FAQs

Can I book a roof inspection without agreeing to repairs?

Yes. An inspection can be booked separately. Confirm the purpose, access, fee and required information before booking; any later repairs need an agreed scope.

Does every roof inspection include a written report and photographs?

Do not assume a standard package. Tell us what documentation you need and confirm the format, included areas and delivery arrangements in the quote before booking.

Can roof photographs identify the exact cause of a leak?

Not on their own. Photographs provide context, but water paths, hidden areas and access limitations may require on-site assessment before a cause and repair scope can be established.

Is a roof inspection the same as a structural or insurance report?

No. A general roof condition inspection is not automatically a structural certification, whole-building inspection or insurer-approved report. Provide the recipient's requirements before booking.

What should I tell you about a damp ceiling?

Describe where and when it appears, the recent weather and any previous roof or equipment work. Share ground-level photographs only if safe; do not climb onto the roof or enter an unsafe roof space.
