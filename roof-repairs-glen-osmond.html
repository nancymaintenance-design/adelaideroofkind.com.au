Common questions

Glen Osmond roof repair questions.

Why does roof slope matter during inspection?

Slope affects water movement and safe access, so it helps shape how the likely entry path and repair approach are assessed.

Can ridge repairs be localised?

Yes, where the surrounding roof is sound. Ellis checks the bedding, pointing and cap position, then quotes local rebedding or repointing rather than assuming the whole ridge needs replacement.

How soon can you respond?

We aim for a response within 30 minutes where roster, access and safety conditions permit.
