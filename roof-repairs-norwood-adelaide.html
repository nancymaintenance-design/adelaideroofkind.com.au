Common questions

Norwood roof repair questions.

When is roof restoration worth considering?

Restoration is worth considering when deterioration extends beyond a local defect and the roof is suitable for repair and preparation. We check the tiles, ridges, flashings and drainage, then quote the repairs, preparation and suitable finishing system.

Can a small leak be repaired without restoring the whole roof?

Yes, when a local repair is suitable for the roof condition. We identify the cause and quote the work needed; broader deterioration may call for a restoration plan.

What helps you diagnose a Norwood roof issue?

Clear notes about where water appears, recent weather, roof material and any known past repairs are useful.
