Common questions

Campbelltown roof repair questions.

Can slipped tiles contribute to a leak?

They can affect the roof covering, but the surrounding overlap, underlay, ridge and flashing details should also be assessed.

What does a leak investigation involve?

We compare the indoor water pattern with the roof coverings, junctions, flashing and drainage on site, trace the entry path and specify the repair work in a written quote.
